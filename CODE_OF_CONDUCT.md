# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming place for people with different
backgrounds, identities, and levels of experience. Please be respectful and
constructive in issues, pull requests, and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and assume good faith.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.
- Do not publish other people's private information, credentials, or logs.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or bad-faith argumentation.
- Publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block contributors who
violate this code. For a confidential report, use the private reporting channel
described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or contact the maintainer through
GitHub. Reports will be reviewed in good faith; retaliation against reporters
is not acceptable.

This code is adapted from the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
